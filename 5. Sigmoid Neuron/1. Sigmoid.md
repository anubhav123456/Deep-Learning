### Lecture Summary: **Sigmoid Neuron – Introduction**

This lecture introduces the **Sigmoid Neuron**, which is one of the most fundamental building blocks of **Deep Neural Networks**.

### Key Points

* **Sigmoid Neuron is the basic unit of Deep Learning.**

  * Just like:

    * A **computer** is the basic unit of a computer network.
    * A **person** is the basic unit of a social network.
  * Similarly, a **Sigmoid Neuron** (or other activation neurons like ReLU) is the basic unit of a deep neural network.

* **Importance**

  * Since deep neural networks are built using sigmoid (or similar) neurons, understanding this concept is essential before learning deep learning.

### Recap of the Previous Lecture (Perceptron)

The instructor reviews what was learned about the **Perceptron**:

* **Inputs**

  * Perceptrons can take **real-valued inputs**.

* **Output**

  * The output is still **Boolean/Binary** (0 or 1), making it suitable for classification.

* **Parameters**

  * A perceptron has:

    * Weights (**w**)
    * Bias (**b**)

* **Decision Boundary**

  * A perceptron creates only a **straight-line decision boundary**.
  * It separates positive and negative examples using a line.

* **Limitation**

  * It works **only for linearly separable data**.
  * It **cannot solve non-linearly separable problems**.

* **Flexibility**

  * By changing weights and bias, different lines (different slopes and intercepts) can be produced, but they are **still only straight lines**.

* **Loss Function**

  * The lecture previously introduced the **0-1 loss**.
  * For Boolean outputs, this is equivalent to the **Squared Error Loss**.

* **Learning Algorithm**

  * The **Perceptron Learning Algorithm** follows a general learning procedure that will also be useful when studying the Sigmoid Neuron.

* **Model Evaluation**

  * The instructor also reviewed how the perceptron model was evaluated.
